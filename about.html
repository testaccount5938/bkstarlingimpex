Who We Are

A Passion for
Exceptional Spaces

Founded in Bengaluru, BK Starling Impex was born from a simple belief: exceptional furniture shouldn't require exceptional compromise. For over 15 years, we have been connecting discerning Indian homeowners and businesses with the world's finest furniture manufacturers.

What began as a specialist import consultancy has grown into one of South India's most trusted luxury furniture sourcing firms. We maintain direct relationships with premium manufacturers across Asia, Europe, and beyond, bypassing layers of markup to deliver outstanding quality at honest prices.

Every client relationship begins with listening. We take time to understand your space, your lifestyle, and your vision, then apply our deep sourcing knowledge to find pieces that don't just fill a room, but complete it. From a single statement piece to a full commercial fit-out, we bring the same commitment to excellence to every project.
