Quick Answers

Frequently Asked

How long does delivery take?

Delivery timelines vary by product and sourcing location. Standard items typically ship within 4–8 weeks. Custom orders may take 8–14 weeks. We provide a precise timeline at the time of order confirmation.

Do you offer customization?

Absolutely. Most of our pieces can be customized in dimensions, upholstery fabric, wood finish, color, and configuration. Share your requirements with us and we'll guide you through the options available.

What areas do you deliver to?

We primarily serve Bengaluru and Karnataka, with delivery available across all major Indian cities. For pan-India delivery, additional logistics charges may apply. Contact us for a delivery quote to your location.

Do you provide installation?

Yes. Our white-glove delivery service includes professional assembly and placement in your space. Our team will ensure every piece is set up correctly and to your satisfaction before they leave your premises.
